Which duplicate products can be merged without a person?

When two shops' catalogues are combined, the same product often appears twice under different titles. Merging two different products is worse than keeping a duplicate. This page scores four ways to find duplicates on public benchmark data, and lets you set the line between merging, a person checking, and keeping them apart.

Set the merge rule

Each candidate pair gets a score from the combined model. Above the merge line it is merged automatically. Below the keep line it stays as two products. In between, a person checks.

Data: the Walmart-Amazon and Abt-Buy product matching benchmarks (Magellan data repository), in the train, validation and test split published with the Ditto research code. The candidate pairs were already filtered to similar looking products, so the share of duplicates is far higher than in a real catalogue.
